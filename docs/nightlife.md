# Nightlife

Checked on 8 November 2026. The gate is unchanged: latitude 18.35 to 18.70 and longitude 73.70 to 74.05, from `src/lib/coords.ts`. Pins are OpenStreetMap objects. A Google place page is only the outward link. Its geometry is not copied onto the pin, and no rating is stored.

The category is a starter. Five rooms, not a directory. High Spirits Cafe stays in Pints. It is the city's main live-music courtyard, and this pass does not move it.

## Included

- **Ballr.** Dance club at Mulik Capital, opposite Trump Towers, Kalyani Nagar. [BookMyShow's venue page](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/c/venues/ballr-pune/mgkn) gives that address and lists Smirnoff Experience club nights. [AllEvents](https://allevents.in/pune/smirnoff-experience-club-nights-pune/200030958068012) places one of those nights on 27 November 2026, with Ankytrixx named for the decks. The pin is the centre of OpenStreetMap way 1410058049, the commercial parcel named Mulik Capital, at 18.5489244, 73.9068773. The parcel spans about 70 metres north–south and about 200 metres east–west, so the dot is the parcel, not a surveyed doorway. It is not the Independence Brewing node at Mulik Palace, about 65 metres west. The Maps place page is titled Ballr - Pune, at Mulik capital, opp. Trump Towers, Kalyani Nagar, Pune 411014. Place id `0x3bc2c1d21df8f8a1:0x92b7539c49c3c500`. That page's own point is about 66 metres from the parcel centre and was not used as the pin.
- **Toy Room.** The [club's own page](https://www.toyroom.co.in/) calls it a dance club inside Sheraton Grand Pune Bund Garden Hotel, Raja Bahadur Motilal Road, and gives the registered address on the ground floor of that hotel. The pin is the centre of OpenStreetMap way 52881227, Sheraton Grand Pune, at 18.5300799, 73.8713386. There is no separate Toy Room node. The Maps page is titled TOYROOM CLUB PUNE, category disco club, located in the Sheraton Grand. Place id `0x3bc2c16c3d9734d9:0x5b8a05b9f0105ac2`. Its point sits about 20 metres from the building centre.
- **2BHK Diner & Key Club.** OpenStreetMap node 11680872748, checked on the node dated 15 March 2026, at 18.5324407, 73.8707620. The node is named 2BHK Diner & Key Club, amenity bar, house number 7, Raja Bahadur Mill Marg, with a note that there is a dance floor. [District's place page](https://www.district.in/dining/pune/2bhk-diner-key-club-bund-garden) puts Unit 7 at Raja Bahadur International Mills, behind the Sheraton Grand, and lists a dance floor, DJ, and live entertainment. [A ticketed night on 6 November 2026](https://www.district.in/events/pataka-sessions-boiler-room-edition-nov6-2026-buy-tickets) was organised by the venue. The Maps page is titled 2BHK Diner & Key Club, at Unit 7, The Mills, behind Sheraton Grand. Place id `0x3bc2c12f212fa8f3:0xfc8dc927cc331f63`. Its point is about 44 metres from the node.
- **Millers The Luxury Club.** OpenStreetMap node 11680882266, checked on the node dated 12 March 2026, at 18.5320998, 73.8705398, named Millers The Luxury Club. The [club's own page](https://millersluxuryclub.co.in/) describes a dance floor at The Mills, behind Sheraton Grand, Sangamvadi. [District](https://www.district.in/dining/pune/millers-the-luxury-club-bund-garden) lists the same mills address and a dance floor, DJ, and live music. The Maps page is titled Millers The Luxury Club, links to millersluxuryclub.co.in, and says beside Social, Sangamvadi. Place id `0x3bc2c1fe823b0ef3:0xa2169562d69ef196`. Its point is about 31 metres from the node. This is a different node from 2BHK, about 45 metres away.
- **The Soundtrack Cafe.** A live-music cafe, not a late dance floor. OpenStreetMap node 12473454971, at 18.5823228, 73.7836778, on New DP Road, Vishal Nagar, Pimpri-Chinchwad. Inside the gate. The [cafe's own page](https://soundtrack.cafe/) says independent artists take the floor, and gives Shop 1–3, Neco Sky Park Commercial, New DP Road, Vishal Nagar, Pimple Nilakh. [Discography's 4 November 2026 roundup](https://discography.in/articles/live-music-venues-pune) lists it for singer-songwriters. The Maps page is titled The Soundtrack Cafe, at Neco SkyPark Commercial, New DP Road, Vishal Nagar, and the blurb mentions indie gigs and open mic nights. Place id `0x3bc2b97f3d267e2f:0x124e7b5863c91db8`. Its point is about 7 metres from the node. The site prints a clock; the card does not treat that clock as a promise.

None of the five cards states a cover charge, a fixed age rule, or a standing weekly timetable. Door rules on the ticket pages belonged to those nights.

## Already on the list, left where they are

- **High Spirits Cafe** is in Pints. [Its own site](https://dahigh.com/) and the Discography roundup describe the Koregaon Park Annexe courtyard as the city's main room for bands and DJ nights. Moving it would hide a place people already find under Pints. The overlap is real: it is nightlife, and it stays a pint card.
- **Toit Pune** is in Pints. Discography also lists acoustic sets there. It stays a brewpub card.
- **Aasmana** is in Date Plans. It is the Ritz-Carlton rooftop, and some club roundups mention DJs. It stays a date card.

## Left out

- **Hard Rock Cafe Pune.** [Mint, 16 March 2026](https://www.livemint.com/news/trends/hard-rock-cafe-shuts-10-restaurants-across-india-after-ending-franchise-deal-only-one-restaurant-to-remain-open-11773668926694.html), reports the Pune cafe among the rooms closed after the franchise ended. Goa was the one left open.
- **Paasha at JW Marriott.** The [hotel's own page](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/pnqmc-jw-marriott-hotel-pune/overview/) says Paasha stays closed for renovation from 1 July to 10 December. On 8 November 2026 that closure was still in force. The same page describes Vault as a members' lounge with cocktails, not as the dance room. Vault was not added in Paasha's place.
- **Penthouze.** Listings agree on a rooftop club at Onyx Tower, next to the Westin in Koregaon Park Annexe, and a Maps-style point sits near the Westin. OpenStreetMap has a Westin hotel way and no object named Onyx or Penthouze. The Westin building is the wrong pin. Left out until the tower itself is mapped.
- **antiSOCIAL, FC Road.** Discography's 4 November 2026 piece still names it for indie gigs, above FC Road Social. BookMyShow has a venue page and, on 8 November 2026, no events loaded. OpenStreetMap has no node for antiSOCIAL or for Cello Platina in the FC Road box that was checked. A neighbourhood dot was not used.
- **Shisha Jazz Cafe.** OpenStreetMap node 283166318 is named Shisha Jazz Cafe on GDP Road in Ghorpuri, near High Spirits, with a check date of 10 March 2026. Discography and an older LBB roundup place Shisha Jazz Cafe at The Mills. A different node at the mills, 11680947135, is named Shisha Cafe and tagged as a hookah lounge. Those are not the same object. Neither pin was used.
- **Asilo.** [asilopune.com](https://www.asilopune.com/) presents a luxury Asian restaurant and cocktail bar at the Westin, open into the evening. That is a dining room, not a dance floor.
- **Club Orilla and Cult House.** Club roundups name them. orilla.in returned an error, and neither name is an OpenStreetMap object inside the gate. No pin, no card.
- **Toto's Garage.** The current Toto's Garage listings are in Bandra, Mumbai. The Liquor Garage on Lane 7, Koregaon Park, is a different room and was not treated as Toto's.
- **Phoenix Marketcity, Mahalaxmi Lawns, and Pandit Farms.** Discography lists them for touring shows and festivals. They are grounds and a mall court, not a room with its own nightly floor.
- **The Mills Social.** Mapped, and it is a bar at the mills. It was not added on top of 2BHK and Millers, which already cover that complex's late rooms.
