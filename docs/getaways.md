# Weekend getaways

The list only pins a place inside the existing gate in `src/lib/coords.ts`: latitude 18.35 to 18.70 and longitude 73.70 to 74.05. That box was not widened. The map has no `maxBounds`; the gate is the data check. The opening camera, near 18.52°N, 73.86°E, is the assumed start for road times. That is central Pune, on the Shivajinagar side of the city centre.

Road times below are published figures for a drive. They are not a promise that every weekend trip finishes inside two hours. A walk or a climb is counted separately from the drive.

## Included

- **Sinhagad Fort.** OpenStreetMap node 2165009895, 18.3651456, 73.7552242, the fort itself, checked against the node on 8 November 2026. Inside the box. [Maharashtra Tourism](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/fort/sinhagad/) says the fort is about 35 km southwest of Pune, the road trip takes about an hour and a half, and the how-to-reach line says 40 km by road. The same page lists a Sinhagad Village Route of about 2.7 km that takes around 1.5–2 hours on foot. That walk is not the drive. The [district page](https://pune.gov.in/en/tourist-place/sinhagad/) says 25 km in the introduction and 35–40 km by road, with buses. Wikipedia’s article was read as background. Its point was not used for the pin. Weekend traffic can run past the published hour and a half.
- **Khadakwasla Dam.** OpenStreetMap way 214776276, checked with its seven nodes on 8 November 2026. The pin is their average, 18.440704, 73.769140, on the dam wall. The way spans about a kilometre along the wall, not the reservoir. Inside the box. The [district page](https://pune.gov.in/en/tourist-place/khadakwasala-dam/) says the dam is 20 km from the city and, in the road line, about 15 km, with buses from Swargate, the railway station, and Shivajinagar. The page does not publish a driving time. A trip of that length from central Pune is usually well inside two hours and can run longer when Sinhagad Road is busy. Wikipedia’s article is a reference only. Its coordinate, 18.453, 73.541, is outside the box and was not the pin.

## Left out because they fall outside the box

Checked on 8 November 2026. These are real trips and were not squeezed in by moving the gate.

- **Bhuleshwar.** Wikipedia’s article point is 18.435874, 74.241081. Longitude 74.24 is east of 74.05. The temple is about 45 km from Pune.
- **Dehu.** Wikipedia’s article point is 18.72, 73.77. Latitude 18.72 is north of 18.70. Dehu Road station is inside the box and is not the town.
- **Khadakwasla’s Wikipedia point.** The article also carries 18.453, 73.541. Longitude 73.54 is west of 73.70, and it does not sit on the mapped dam. The pin uses the dam way instead.
- **Panshet, Varasgaon, Mulshi, Lavasa, Lonavala, Rajgad, and Torna** sit west or south of the box on the usual maps of those places. They were not given pins.

## Left out inside the box

- **Alandi** is inside the box, about 22 km north, and the pilgrimage is real. The OpenStreetMap hit is the village centroid, not Sant Dnyaneshwar’s samadhi. A town dot is not the door.
- **Dive Ghat** inside the box is the highway, not a viewpoint.
- **Peacock Bay** and **Sinhagad Valley** are separate mapped features on the same southwest road. They were not added on top of the fort and the dam.
